### 1. Executive Summary

In response to architectural requirements, the Aegis64 kernel has been finalized with:
1. **Decoupled Dedicated Storage Hierarchy:**
   - Storage devices are completely removed from generic `/dev` and assigned dedicated, type-safe VFS canonical paths:
     - `/ssd/ssd1`, `/ssd/ssd2` (Solid State Drives - NVMe PCIe & AHCI SATA SSD)
     - `/hdd/hdd1`, `/hdd/hdd2` (Hard Disk Drives - ATA/IDE PIO & SATA Rotational HDD)
     - `/usb/usb1`, `/usb/usb2` (USB 3.x Flash Mass Storage)
     - `/sdxc/card1` (High-Capacity SDXC Flash Cards)
     - `/sdhc/card1` (Standard SDHC Flash Cards)
   - `/dev` is strictly reserved for character & pseudo devices (`null`, `zero`, `console`).
2. **AegisShell 2.5: MS-DOS & Base Linux POSIX Hybrid CLI Engine:**
   - Full MS-DOS command set: `dir`, `cd`, `chdir`, `type`, `copy`, `ren`, `move`, `del`, `erase`, `md`, `mkdir`, `rd`, `rmdir`, `tree`, `cls`, `ver`, `date`, `time`, `mem`.
   - Full Base Linux POSIX command set: `ls [-l]`, `pwd`, `cat`, `head [-n]`, `tail [-n]`, `cp`, `mv`, `rm`, `touch`, `mkdir`, `rmdir`, `stat`, `grep`, `find`, `wc`, `dump/hexdump/xxd`, `free`, `ps`, `tasks`, `df`, `whoami`, `echo [> | >>]`.
   - Full Natural Expressive English command phrasing: `copy <src> to <dst>`, `move <src> to <dst>`, `delete <file>`, `read <file>`, `show <cat>`, `ping to <ip>`, `remount volume as <mode>`, etc.
3. **USB 3.x Mass Storage & Flash Card Subsystem:**
   - Integrated `UsbMassStorageDevice` and `SdCardDevice` drivers in `src/drivers/storage/`.
   - Automatic registration and block I/O support through VFS.
